The madness that lurked within the eyes of Ivan the Terrible was a chasm so deep, it devoured the souls of those around him. His son's lifeless gaze seemed to sear itself into the frozen moment, as if begging for forgiveness from the father who had brought about his downfall.

Ivan's hand trembled, his fingers digging deep into the wound on his son's temple. The young prince's arm lay limp on the carpet, a stark contrast to the desperate attempt to hold him upright. The dimly lit chamber seemed to shrink, trapping the two figures in an eternal agony. Regret had come too late, and in that instant, Ivan was consumed by the darkness he had unleashed.

In this moment of shattered tranquility, the weight of his actions crushed him, a cold reminder that the terror he had sown would never be silenced. Time stood still, and in its silence, Ivan's madness was revealed.
