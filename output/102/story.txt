Goya’s brush captures the exact moment the Titan’s teeth tear through soft, pale flesh, turning myth into a visceral scream of paternal ruin. Focus on those bulging, frantic eyes—the whites wide with a madness that recognizes no kin—as his elongated fingers dig deep into the mangled, headless torso of his own lineage. The darkness swallows the world, leaving only the sickening contrast of arterial red staining a mouth that has traded future legacy for the immediate, hollow comfort of cannibalism. Power does not protect; it only consumes its own foundation to stave off the inevitable silence of oblivion. He destroys his bloodline to cheat a prophecy, yet the void in his hollow gaze proves that when you devour your own future, you are left only with ghosts.
