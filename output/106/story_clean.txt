The colossal, hollowed-out ribcage of Utagawa Kuniyoshi’s nightmare descends, a mountain of bleached bone summoned by Takiyasha the Witch to swallow the living whole. Look at the warriors’ dilated pupils, their knuckles white as they grip sword hilts that offer no defense against a death this massive and ancient. The skeletal hand, a jagged architecture of phalanges, reaches from the void to claim the center of the ruined palace, dwarfing the frantic scrolls and layered silk robes. Panic is a silent scream caught in the throat of the doomed, realizing that flesh is merely a fragile, temporary casing for the marrow-deep hunger of the grave. You are not witnessing a battle, but a reclamation by the inevitable. In the end, your skeleton is simply waiting for its turn to stand.
